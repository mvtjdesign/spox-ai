Common Queries.

What is the primary utility of SPOX?

SPOX functions as the native compute credit for the reasoning protocol. It is used to settle computation costs, reward node operators, and facilitate governance within the DAO.

How does the reasoning verification work?

The protocol utilizes a Zero-Knowledge proof system where nodes process AI requests and return a cryptographic "receipt" that proves the logic was executed as intended without exposing the raw data.

Is the protocol restricted to certain jurisdictions?

Access to the protocol and token allocation may be restricted in specific regions (e.g., USA, China) to ensure global regulatory compliance. Refer to our legal documentation for details.

Can developers build on the spox AI layer?

Yes. We will release a comprehensive SDK and developer portal during Phase 2, enabling the integration of autonomous agents and smart contract automation.
